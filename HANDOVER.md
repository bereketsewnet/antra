## 3. What We Delivered — Features Summary

### Responsive Design
The website automatically adapts to all screen sizes:

| Device | Experience |
|---|---|
| 📱 **Mobile Phone** | Fully optimized — touch-friendly buttons, vertical layouts, mobile menu, optimized images |
| 📱 **Tablet** | Adaptive grid — larger touch targets, balanced typography |
| 💻 **Desktop** | Full visual experience — multi-column layouts, hover effects, smooth animations |
| 🖥️ **Large Screens** | Maintains readability and visual hierarchy at any width |

### Light & Dark Mode
The site offers two themes that visitors can toggle from the navigation bar:

- ☀️ **Light Mode** — Bright, clean, professional (default)
- 🌙 **Dark Mode** — Modern, premium, easier on the eyes
- ✨ The site remembers each visitor's preference automatically

### Performance & Speed
The website is built using modern web technology (React + Vite) and optimized for speed:

- ⚡ **Fast Loading** — Code is split, minified, and compressed (gzip)
- 🖼️ **Optimized Images** — WebP format with long-term browser caching
- 🚀 **Smooth Animations** — GPU-accelerated with Lenis smooth scroll & Framer Motion
- 📦 **Total Page Size** — ~207 KB gzipped JavaScript, 13 KB gzipped CSS

### Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
Comprehensive SEO setup to help your business appear on Google and other search engines:

- ✅ Unique titles, descriptions, and keywords on every page
- ✅ Ethiopian market keywords ("management consulting Addis Ababa", "construction equipment supplier Ethiopia", etc.)
- ✅ Open Graph tags (for sharing on Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp)
- ✅ Twitter Card support
- ✅ Geographic targeting (Ethiopia, Addis Ababa)
- ✅ Structured Data (JSON-LD) for rich search results
- ✅ XML Sitemap submitted at `/sitemap.xml`
- ✅ Robots.txt configured
- ✅ Canonical URLs for proper indexing

### Security
- 🔒 **Forced HTTPS** — All traffic encrypted with SSL
- 🔒 **Security Headers** — X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy
- 🔒 **Spam Protection** — Honeypot field + IP rate-limiting on contact form
- 🔒 **Server-Side Email** — SMTP credentials safely hidden, never exposed to visitors

### Working Contact Form
A fully functional contact form connected to your email account. Submissions are delivered directly to `info@antragroup.et` with full sender details.

### Smooth Navigation
- 🔄 **Single-Page Application (SPA)** — Instant page transitions without reloading
- 🎯 **Auto Scroll-to-Top** — Every page navigation starts from the top
- 📱 **Mobile-Friendly Menu** — Slide-out menu on small screens

## 5. Email System

### Active Email Account
The website's contact form is wired to send all messages to your business email.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Email Address** | `info@antragroup.et` |
| **Display Name** | Antra Business Group |
| **Response Time** | 1 business day (promised on website) |

### Email Configuration (Secure SSL/TLS)

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| **Username** | `info@antragroup.et` |
| **Password** | Use the email account password (provided separately and securely) |
| **Incoming Server (IMAP)** | `mail.antragroup.et` — Port `993` |
| **Incoming Server (POP3)** | `mail.antragroup.et` — Port `995` |
| **Outgoing Server (SMTP)** | `mail.antragroup.et` — Port `465` |
| **Authentication** | Required for IMAP, POP3, and SMTP |

### How to Access Your Email

You can read and send emails in three ways:

1. **Webmail (browser, any device)**
   Visit `https://antragroup.et:2096` or `https://antragroup.et/webmail` and log in with the credentials above.

2. **Mobile Phone (Gmail / Outlook / Mail app)**
   Add a new email account using the IMAP/SMTP settings above.

3. **Desktop App (Outlook / Thunderbird / Apple Mail)**
   Same IMAP/SMTP settings — most apps configure automatically once you enter the email address and password.

### Setting Up Email on Your Phone
1. Open your email app (Gmail, Outlook, Mail)
2. Add Account → "Other" or "IMAP"
3. Enter `info@antragroup.et` and the password
4. When prompted for server settings, enter the values from the table above
5. Done — your inbox will sync automatically

### Contact Form Behavior
When a visitor submits the form on `https://antragroup.et/contact`:

1. The form sends the data securely to your server
2. The server validates the information (real email, non-empty message, etc.)
3. An email is composed and sent to `info@antragroup.et`
4. The email includes the visitor's name, email, phone, company, area of interest, and message
5. You can **reply directly** to the email — your reply goes straight to the visitor
6. The visitor sees a "Message received" confirmation on the page

### Need Help Configuring Email?
If you need help connecting your email to your phone, computer, or any email client, **please reach out to us at Wubsites — we are happy to walk you through it at no extra charge.**
