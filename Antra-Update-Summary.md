# Antra Business Group Website — Update Summary

**Prepared for:** Antra Business Group
**Prepared by:** Wubsites Development Team
**Date:** July 11, 2026

---

## Overview

This document summarizes the improvements made to the Antra Business Group website in this update cycle. The work covered every page of the site — Home, About, Consultancy, Trading, and Contact — along with sitewide navigation and performance improvements. All changes are complete and ready for review.

---

## Homepage

- Rewrote the "What We Do" section with updated messaging and removed content that was duplicated elsewhere on the page.
- Redesigned the Services section with a cleaner, more modern card layout.
- Made the animated 3D cube interactive — visitors can now click it to jump straight to the relevant section on the Consultancy page.
- Updated the floating labels around the cube to read "Challenges we help you solve."
- Added a "Latest News" section featuring the OVID partnership announcement.
- Consolidated leadership content into a new, cleaner "Our Team" section.
- Improved page loading speed by optimizing images and background media.

## About Us Page

- Reorganized the page so "Our Story," "Our Mission," and "Core Values" now flow in a clear, logical order.
- Removed content that was repeated across multiple sections.
- Corrected the mission statement wording to match the approved copy exactly.
- Combined the "Ready to Work With Us" call-to-action with the "Find Us" section for smoother reading.

## Consultancy Page

- Reordered the page for better flow: Our Practices, Strategy, What Makes Us Unique, then the client-challenges section, followed by the call-to-action and Key Partners.
- Replaced a static image with a clear, animated list outlining our strategic approach.
- Added a new "What Makes Us Unique" section highlighting five key strengths, each with its own icon.
- Simplified the interactive service cards for a cleaner, more consistent experience.

## Trading Page

- Added a new "What Makes Us Unique" section covering distribution expertise, brand building, and fleet/trader relationships.
- Reorganized "Why Buyers Work With Us" and "Key Suppliers" for a smoother page flow.
- Completed the "Our Approach" section with its full intended copy.
- Simplified the product category cards for a cleaner presentation.

## Contact Page

- Fixed a display issue where the hero background image did not fully cover the page.
- The contact form now shows clear, specific guidance when something needs correcting (e.g. "Please enter a valid email address") instead of one generic error message.
- Updated business hours to Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, and Saturday (half day), 8:00 AM – 12:00 PM.
- Updated the map and directions links to point to the exact office location.

## Sitewide Improvements

- **New navigation menus:** hovering over About, Consultancy, or Trading in the main menu now reveals direct links to every section of that page, so visitors can jump straight to what they're looking for.
- **Light Mode fixes:** resolved numerous display issues across the site where text was difficult or impossible to read in Light Mode.
- **Reliability fix:** corrected an issue where certain in-page links could occasionally land in the wrong place; navigation now lands precisely on target every time.
- **Footer cleanup:** removed a redundant duplicate link and corrected an abbreviated service name to its full form.
- **General performance improvements** across all pages.

---

*All changes described above are complete on the development build. We recommend a full walkthrough of each page — in both Dark and Light Mode — before this update goes live.*
